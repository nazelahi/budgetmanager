# Privacy Policy for Money Pro

**Last Updated: December 2024**

## Introduction

Money Pro ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

Money Pro is designed to be a local-first application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. All data remains on your device.

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following types of data are stored locally on your device using secure local storage:

#### Financial Data

- **Transactions**: Amount, description, category, type (income/expense), and date
- **Categories**: Custom category names, types, colors, and icons
- **Budgets**: Budget amounts, periods, and spending limits
- **Financial Statistics**: Monthly income, expenses, and balance calculations

#### App Settings and Preferences

- **Currency Settings**: Your preferred currency (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.)
- **Theme Preferences**: Light or dark mode selection
- **Notification Settings**: Alert preferences and quiet hours
- **Alert Thresholds**: Custom warning percentages for budget alerts

#### User-Generated Content

- **Custom Categories**: Categories you create with custom names, colors, and icons
- **Transaction Descriptions**: Personal notes and descriptions for transactions
- **Budget Plans**: Your personal budget allocations and financial goals

## How We Use Your Information

Since all data is stored locally on your device, we do not have access to your information. The data is used solely for:

- **App Functionality**: Providing budget tracking, transaction management, and financial analytics
- **Personalization**: Customizing the app experience based on your settings and preferences
- **Data Visualization**: Generating charts, reports, and financial insights
- **Notifications**: Sending budget alerts and reminders based on your settings

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage

- All data is stored locally on your device using React Native AsyncStorage
- Data is encrypted at the device level using the device's built-in security features
- No data is transmitted to external servers or third-party services

### Data Export and Backup

- You can export your data in JSON or CSV format for backup purposes
- Export files are generated locally and can be saved to your device or shared at your discretion
- We do not have access to exported data

### Data Deletion

- You can delete all app data through the app's settings
- Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data
- We cannot recover deleted data as it was never transmitted to our servers

## Third-Party Services

Money Pro uses the following third-party services that may collect certain information:

### Expo Services

- **Expo Updates**: For app updates and bug fixes
- **Expo Analytics**: Basic app usage analytics (anonymized)
- **Expo Crash Reporting**: Error reporting to improve app stability

### React Native Libraries

- **AsyncStorage**: For local data persistence
- **Vector Icons**: For UI icons and graphics
- **Chart Kit**: For data visualization (no data transmission)

## Permissions

Money Pro may request the following permissions:

### iOS Permissions

- **Face ID/Touch ID**: For app authentication and security (optional)
- **Notifications**: For budget alerts and reminders (optional)

### Android Permissions

- **Biometric Authentication**: For app security (optional)
- **Notification Access**: For budget alerts (optional)
- **File Access**: For data export functionality (optional)

## Children's Privacy

Money Pro is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to third parties because:

1. All data is stored locally on your device
2. We do not have access to your personal information
3. No data is transmitted to our servers or external services

## International Users

Since all data is stored locally on your device, this privacy policy applies regardless of your location. Your data remains on your device and is subject to your local data protection laws.
